First Fruits Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 24, 2026

First Fruits is designed so your journal remains private on your iPhone.

Data First Fruits stores

First Fruits stores your Scripture, Observe, Interpret, Apply, and Pray entries in the app’s local SwiftData database on your device. The SwiftData journal is local-only. The app also stores local settings needed to remember your morning schedule, selected app-shield tokens, and whether the morning entry was completed or bypassed.

Data First Fruits does not collect or share

First Fruits does not send journal text, selected-app identities, or usage history to the developer. Version 1.0 does not share journal entries or accountability status off device. It has no custom account system, advertising, analytics SDK, remote AI provider, or iCloud sharing or sync.

On-device intelligence

When available, reflective follow-up questions use Apple’s on-device Foundation Models framework. When unavailable, First Fruits uses deterministic prompts generated in the app. Journal text is not sent to a remote AI service.

Family Controls

First Fruits uses Apple’s Family Controls, Managed Settings, and Device Activity frameworks after the device owner authorizes access. The user chooses which apps, categories, and websites to shield. First Fruits does not use these frameworks for covert monitoring or to collect usage reports.

Accountability and subscriptions

Accountability sharing is unavailable in First Fruits 1.0. Subscriptions are unavailable in First Fruits 1.0. The app does not invite partners or share completion, bypass, missed, or other status records.

Deletion

Settings → Privacy → Delete journal data removes entries and drafts from the local SwiftData database. Deleting the app removes its local journal data according to iOS behavior.

Candidate scope

This policy describes the local First Fruits 1.0 (2) source candidate. It is not evidence that this candidate has been signed, uploaded, processed by TestFlight, or published. Those release states require separately identified artifact and App Store Connect evidence.
