First Fruits Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 9, 2026

First Fruits is designed so your journal remains private on your iPhone.

Data stored on your device

First Fruits stores your Scripture, Observe, Interpret, Apply, and Pray responses locally on your device. The app also stores your selected app restrictions, morning schedule, completion state, emergency-release state, and partial draft locally or in an Apple app group shared only with First Fruits extensions.

Data First Fruits does not collect

First Fruits does not send journal text, selected-app identities, or usage history to the developer. Version 1.0 has no custom account system, advertising, analytics SDK, remote AI provider, or CloudKit journal sync.

On-device intelligence

When available, reflection prompts are generated with Apple's on-device Foundation Models framework. Journal text is not sent to an external AI provider. A deterministic on-device fallback is used when Foundation Models is unavailable.

Screen Time and Family Controls

If you grant Family Controls authorization, First Fruits uses Apple's FamilyControls, ManagedSettings, and DeviceActivity frameworks to shield only the apps and categories you select during your configured morning window. These selections are not sent to the developer.

Accountability

Accountability sharing is optional and uses Apple CloudKit sharing. If the initiating user has an active accountability subscription and chooses to invite one partner, First Fruits stores only an occurrence date, scheduled window, state (completed, bypassed, or missed), event time, and schema version in that user's private iCloud database. Apple shares only those records with the invited iCloud participant. Journal text, AI prompts and responses, selected-app identities, and free-form bypass reasons are never included. The owner can manage or stop the iCloud share at any time. Supporting an invited partner does not require a subscription.

Deletion

Use Settings → Delete journal data to remove entries and drafts stored by First Fruits. Removing the app also removes its local app data. Manage or stop an accountability share through the accountability screen.
